Manual & Explained Method of the Simulation

Working from the logic of supply and demand in real estate development, this simulation explores how policy design could challenge common assumptions. The two main actors are developers and tenants; the simulation acts as the government or policymaker. Move policy tiles into Policy Metrics to build a scenario. Drag a tile by its arrow, or click the arrow to add or remove it. The base condition stays fixed. Press play to advance slowly; dragging the play button or timeline pauses playback. This first version demonstrates the interface with illustrative values. The spatial model and research data will be connected in the next phase.
